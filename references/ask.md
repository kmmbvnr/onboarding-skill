# Decide whether to ask a buddy

First ask the learner to write:

- the goal;
- the expected result;
- the observed result;
- what they tried;
- their current explanation;
- the decision or fact they need.

Inspect safe local evidence when it can answer the question. Do not read or reveal secrets.

If one clear local check can answer the question, give that check first. Do not draft a message yet. Reassess after the learner reports the result.

Ask a buddy when the missing fact concerns product intent, ownership, policy, access, historical constraints, production state, or a team decision.

Draft the message in the learner's language. Apply ASD-STE100 principles. Do not invent attempts or results.

```text
Hi <name>. I need help with <specific question>.

Goal: <goal>
Expected: <expected result>
Observed: <observed result>
Tried: <short list>
My current understanding: <one testable explanation>
Question: <one question that enables the next action>
```

Remove empty fields. Keep the final message under 180 words.
